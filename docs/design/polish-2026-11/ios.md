# iOS polish audit (November 2026 program)

Owner: iOS polish (all of `ui/`). Bar: the November 2026 quality bar (`docs/design/quality-bar.md`). Review protocol: [design-review.md](../design-review.md).

Date: November 26, 2026. Method: a Debug build of `main` at `bdaffb9f`, launched with `--mock-member` and the review flags, captured on the Claude-Base simulator (iPhone, 402 x 874 pt, iOS 27.2) in English, Spanish, XXXL and AX3 text, plus interactive flows (chat, photo viewer, invite sheet, onboarding, scrolled pages, a held press), and on Claude-Duo folded (outer display) and open (inner display). A read-only code inventory measured press styles, spinners, error views, motion curves, refresh, swipe actions, formatting, fixed frames, touch targets and haptics. Earlier work is in [docs/quality/ios-audit-2026-11.md](../../quality/ios-audit-2026-11.md); items fixed there are not repeated.

Effort: S (under 2 h), M (half a day), L (a day or more). Wave: the PR that addresses it (W1 = design-system interaction and state primitives, W2 = screen-level work, Later = not in this program, reason given).

## Summary

The app's visuals are close to the Figma reference and most states exist, but it feels less finished than the admin site because of the layer between the pixels: what happens when you touch, wait, scroll and fail. Cards wash out toward transparent when pressed. Scrolled content cuts off with a hard edge under the fixed header and slides under the floating bar with no separation. Cold start and route loads show a bare system spinner ("Loading…") on white, and shell error paths are stock system views with an em dash in the copy. Loading skeletons exist on some screens and are flat gray boxes on others. Photo cards on My Events have no readability gradient while the same cards on Home do. Several review flags do nothing in the `--mock-member` app, so the event page's Join, Full and Cancelled states could not be reviewed in the real app. Above the component layer, the event page hides when, where and cost below the fold, cards do not feel live (no "Tomorrow", no spots left) and section headers use two unrelated type styles.

## Audit review

An independent Opus reviewer read the first draft against the quality bar and the screenshots (verdict: needs changes). Accepted: TOOL-1 to the top (event states were never seen), VIS-1 up, press styles split into card, row and icon variants instead of one scale for everything, the bottom scrim reduced to a short fade instead of a 140 pt white wash over photos, route-aware skeletons instead of one generic page skeleton, and new items EVT-1, DYN-1, NOT-1, TYPE-1, CON-1, VIS-7 and the failure variant of the empty state. Checked and rejected: the 24-hour times (the simulator sets `AppleICUForce24HourTime`; the app formats by locale). Deferred: the brand launch image (needs a logo asset in the app catalog and a Release measurement).

## Not changed (by design)

- Locked decisions: tinted info cards, purple buttons with white text, Home toggle color, naming rules.
- Figma-derived geometry of the floating bar and headers stays; the fades sit behind them.
- Fixture content (event descriptions with double spaces, English notification bodies in Spanish) is server or fixture text, not UI copy.

## Wave log

### Wave 1: design-system interaction and state primitives

Done: TOOL-1, IX-1, VIS-1, LAY-1, LAY-2, ST-1, ST-2, ST-3, ST-4, DUO-1 (fade only), VIS-5 (failure variant), CPY-1 (dashes), IX-4 (Rewards; `DIPHaptics.navigate()` already plays nothing and the challenge stepper tick is a value change, so both stay), MOT-1 (`DIPMotion.press`; the challenge pulse and interest shimmer already stop under Reduce Motion, so no change was needed).

- Press: `DIPCardPressStyle` (photo and hero cards: 0.985 scale, 12 % ink overlay, spring), `DIPIconPressStyle` (Back, Close, header bell and avatar, Duo rail), `DIPRowPressStyle` (available for list rows), `DIPMotion.press`.
- Edges: `dipTopEdgeFade()` on page scroll views (appears only once scrolled), `DIPEdgeFade.floatingBar` behind the phone bar and the Duo action bar, `DIPPhotoScrim` shared by Home, My Events and memories.
- States: `DIPPageSkeleton` (tab, detail, rows, grid) for first load, routes, album and notification settings; invitation-card skeleton; `DIPEmptyState(tone: .failure)` on every load failure; shell failure, My Events unavailable and Activity unavailable use the shared empty state; `DIPPausedPill` replaces "Updates paused — Retry".
- Review fixes (design review rounds): Back stays real over the loading skeleton; the gray unavailable bar ("Activity full", "Activity cancelled") uses ink text (white on #CCCCCC was 1.6:1); the cancelled page keeps the date pill's format and glyph, drops Directions and the tappable Where row; the shell failure reads "Something went wrong" with the specific reason under it and sits where the first section would be; the failure glyph is a neutral exclamation mark, not a Wi-Fi symbol, because not every failure is offline.
- Review flags (Debug): `--shell-state=loading|failed`; `--empty-state`, `--event-full`, `--event-cancelled` and `--screen=event` now work in the `--mock-member` app.
- Reviewer findings not taken in wave 1 (P3, carried to wave 2): the bar fade lightens the bottom of strongly colored reward cards; half-covered pills above the bar at rest; the tab skeleton's left pill is shorter than the real title pill; the detail skeleton has no Invite/Share row; Home empty has no next step; "Activity full" offers no alternative.

### Wave 2: screen level

Done: DYN-1 (`DIPEventDateText` relative dates on cards, absolute with the year for memories), EVT-1 (facts line with time range, venue, cost or points under the title; description clamped to three lines with Read more; "See what changed" and "Leave Activity" as quiet `DIPTextActionRow`s), NOT-1 (Invitations is the one solid control, "Mark all as read" a text action on the first day header, swipe left to mark one notification read), TYPE-1 (colon removed; the wider header style question stays open), VIS-3 (Sign out as a destructive text action), wave-1 P3 (bar fade 36 to 24 pt).
