How to join Open the app

Share and earn

Add photos to the event album and collect points together.

How to join

  1. Your company joins the beta

    During the private beta, companies join DIP with us. Organizers then plan events in the admin portal.

  2. You get an invitation

    Your DIP admin adds you to your company's roster and can send you an invitation by email.

  3. Install and sign in

    Ask your admin for the iPhone beta, then sign in with Google using your work email.
