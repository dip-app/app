Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

DIP is an app that helps companies organize events, see who is going, share photos and earn points as a team. This policy explains what DIP collects, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name, email address and profile photo from your Google account when you sign inTo create your account and show you to coworkers
Company membership (your company, role, and roster entry)To decide which events and people you can see
Interests, RSVPs, invitations, points and challenge entriesTo run events, rewards and weekly challenges
Content you post: event photos, comments, chat messages, challenge photosTo share them with the people in your company who can see that event
Bug reports you send: your message, an optional screenshot, and diagnostics (app version, device model, iOS version, current screen)To fix problems; visible to your company's administrators and the DIP team
Push notification token (only if you allow notifications)To send reminders and updates you asked for
Your email notification choices and a delivery record for each email (which notification, when, and whether it was delivered, bounced or marked as spam)To send the email notifications you keep on, avoid duplicates, and stop emailing addresses that bounce or complain

What DIP can access on your device

  • Photos: you choose the photos you upload with the system photo picker; DIP can also save photos to your library when you tap Save.
  • Calendar: to add an event, DIP opens the iOS event editor and you review and save it; this needs no calendar permission. If you turn on the optional “Show calendar conflicts” setting (Profile, Calendar; off by default), iOS asks for full calendar access. DIP then reads event times, titles and free/busy status from the calendars on that device, only to warn you about overlaps and to save, update or remove the entries DIP itself added. Calendar data is processed only on your device: it is never uploaded, stored on DIP servers, shared, sold, or used for ads or analytics. DIP keeps on the device only the identifiers and times of the entries it added. Conflicts with other DIP events you joined are worked out from DIP data the app already has. You can turn the setting off in Profile or revoke access in iOS Settings, DIP, Calendars at any time.
  • Notifications: only if you allow them in the iOS prompt. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Who can see your information

Other people at your company can see your name, photo, the events you joined and content you post to those events. Administrators of your company can see its member list, roster, event attendance, submissions and bug reports. We do not share your information with other companies.

Service providers

DIP runs on Google Firebase and Google Cloud (sign-in, database, file storage, server functions, App Check and push messaging) and uses Apple's push notification and device attestation services. These providers process data on our behalf to run the app.

Email notifications (invitations, event updates, reminders you turn on, challenge reviews) are sent to the email address from your Google account. When email is turned on for your company, an email delivery provider processes your address and the message on our behalf; we name that provider here before the first email is sent. Emails never contain chat messages, photos or comments, and we do not track opens or clicks.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Photos and posts stay with the events they belong to. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data tied to it; some records, such as audit logs kept by your company's administrators, may be retained as needed for security and legal reasons.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting providers. Access is limited by your company membership and enforced on the server. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

DIP is for working adults and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing us.
  • Turn off notifications, photo or calendar access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Unsubscribe from any email notification with the link at the bottom of the email, or change email settings in the app under Profile > Notifications.
  • Stop using DIP at any time by signing out and asking us to delete your account.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
