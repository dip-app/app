Support

Contact us

Email support@letsdip.work and tell us what happened, what device and iOS version you use, and what you expected to happen.

Quick answers

  • I can't sign in. DIP signs in with a Google account. Use your work email. If your company's domain isn't recognized yet, ask your DIP administrator to check the company setup.
  • I don't see any events. Pull down on Home to refresh. Events are only shown to people at your company.
  • Something looks broken. Shake your phone to send a bug report with a screenshot straight from the app.
  • Photos won't upload. Check your connection and tap retry on the photo. Uploads continue when you are back online.
  • Delete my account or data. Email us from your sign-in address and we will take care of it. See the Privacy Policy.
