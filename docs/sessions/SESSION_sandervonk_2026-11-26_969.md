## Changes

- Shared layout (layout.ts): header and footer art is one transparent image per band (brand-purple mark and wordmark, faint clouds, mascots on a white card inside the body card's edge), so light, dark and forced inversion all work without a light/dark swap; dark bands use the dark page colour (two tones instead of three); invisible padding after the preheader; a hairline above the footer and tighter footer rows; struck-through old values darker (#6B7080, about 5:1).
- Event emails (render.ts): a small uppercase kicker with the state ("Event updated", "Event canceled" in red with a lighter red in dark mode), then the event title as the Fjalla heading (smaller for titles over 60 characters), then the lead and When/Where table; the "Updated" pill and the bold title row are gone. Plain text no longer prints the "[Updated]" line (the heading line says it).
- Preheaders: an overlong title, theme or company name is shortened at a word boundary with an ellipsis instead of cutting the finished sentence ("Join in with your."); the day now reads "(Fri, Nov 20)".
- Admin ideas: outlined "Start this event" buttons are 44 px tall links; heading spacing matches.
- Assets: apps/admin/public/img/email/header.png and footer.png regenerated by scripts/generate-email-assets.sh; -dark copies kept for emails already sent.
- Tests and goldens: gcp/tests/unit/email-templates.test.ts, email-admin-suggestions.test.ts, all snapshots regenerated with UPDATE_EMAIL_SNAPSHOTS=1. Docs: docs/design/stretch/email.md style guide, apps/admin/public/img/email/README.md.

English string diffs (i18n/en.ts):
- `email.preheader.onDay`: "{sentence} on {day}." to "{sentence} ({day})." ("was canceled on Fri" read as the cancellation date).
- `email.weeklyTheme.preheader`: "{title} is the new weekly theme. Join in with your coworkers." to "Runs {dates}. Join in with coworkers, then log what you did in DIP." (no longer repeats the subject).
- `email.bugResolved.heading`: "Thanks for your report" to "Problem resolved" (the lead already thanks).
- `email.ideas.footer.reason`, `email.adminDigest.reason`: "You get this because" to "You are getting this because" (one footer voice).
Spanish: matching preheader and heading strings, and "Configurar en el portal de administración" instead of the repetitive "Administrar en el portal de administración".
