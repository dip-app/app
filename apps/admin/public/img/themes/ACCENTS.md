# Weekly theme accent backgrounds

Art files are transparent (730x539, same slot as `fitness.png`); the card draws the solid accent behind them.
Use these hex values as each theme's `accent`. The title sits bottom-left: use ink text on yellow, green, blue, teal and coral backgrounds and white on ink and purple; check contrast per accent when wiring.

| outdoors | Outdoors | #6BE5D0 | teal |
| food | Food & Drink | #FF7C73 | coral |
| arts | Arts & Crafts | #7B88FA | purple |
| games | Games | #FFCC3E | yellow |
| music | Music | #66D9FF | blue |
| learning | Learning | #6BFF9B | green |
| volunteering | Volunteering | #1E212B | ink |
| wellness | Wellness | #FF7C73 | coral (not adjacent to Food in picker order) |
